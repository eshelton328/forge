    fix(esp32s3-devkit): connect VBAT to Q1 drain — board shipped with a dead battery input (#118)
    
    The assembled boards do not power on. Root cause: Q1's drain pad (/VBAT, the
    battery input) has no copper on it, so the battery never reaches the reverse-
    protection FET. Confirmed on hardware — injecting battery+ at the /PFET net
    downstream of Q1 boots the board normally.
    
    How it happened:
    - The entire battery path (/VBAT, /PFET, /VBAT_SW) was routed with copper pours
      and ZERO tracks.
    - The /VBAT pour is pinched to 0.17 mm against the neighbouring /PFET pour right
      where Q1's drain sits, so the fill never reached the pad.
    - KiCad DRC reported "0 unconnected items" on every run: the ratsnest treats a
      pad as zone-connected when the zone OUTLINE overlaps it, even though the
      FILLED copper does not.
    
    Fix: route /VBAT explicitly, J1.2 -> Q1.3, 0.8 mm on F.Cu, inside the existing
    VBAT corridor. /PFET and /VBAT_SW are left as-is: both verified to have their
    pads inside filled copper, and proven conducting on the hardware.
    
    Guard so this cannot ship again: scripts/ci/check_copper_connectivity.py asserts
    the real invariant geometrically — every pad on a multi-pad net must be inside
    same-net filled copper or touched by a same-net track/via. Pads sharing a number
    in one footprint (module thermal pads with heatsink vias, switch double pins)
    count as one node. Wired into `make check` and the CI drc job.
    
    Verified: the guard FAILS on the pre-fix board naming exactly Q1.3, and PASSES
    on the fixed board and on tps63070-breakout. ERC, DRC, parity, fab-DRC all pass.
