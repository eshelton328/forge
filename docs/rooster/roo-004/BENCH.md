# Cube bench acceptance specification — ROO-004 v1

September 7, 2026. Applies to source R1 identified in [README.md](README.md), then to explicitly identified manufactured units built from a reviewed release. **All B01–B15 cases are NOT RUN.** ROO-011 turns this into diagnostic firmware and confirms available equipment before physical execution. Counts, dwell times and load steps below define a proposed engineering sampling plan; they do not set Erik's product defaults or promise reliability.

## Recording and decision rules

Record unit/lot and all three PCB revisions; release hash; populated substitutions/DNPs; cell/holder/harness/module identities; cable lengths; diagnostic firmware/build hash; instrument models, calibration/range/bandwidth and uncertainty; ambient temperature; setup photo; actual test conditions; readings/waveform filenames; failure/retest history. Attach raw evidence to [bench-results.csv](bench-results.csv), using a copy or added per-unit rows. Do not replace NOT RUN with PASS on the strength of a render, source test or simulation.

- **PASS/FAIL** applies only to an explicit functional rule or a frozen electrical limit at the stated condition. For a range `[L,U]`, require `measured_min − uncertainty ≥ L` and `measured_max + uncertainty ≤ U`; an overlap is INCONCLUSIVE. For upper/lower-only limits use the corresponding side. Values during the intentional startup/shutdown transition are separately characterized; a powered operating load must stay in its allowed range.
- **CHARACTERIZED** means data collected without an agreed performance pass limit. Attach the curve and send the decision to D06/ROO-001; it does not count as product PASS. A measured hard-limit excursion still fails the applicable electrical gate.
- **BLOCKED** names the missing instrument, chosen part, diagnostic or limit. Tests may be split into available functional checks and blocked waveform/current checks. Do not infer D08 inventory from local software tools.
- Repeat functional bring-up on each assembled set. The load/endurance/RF experiment begins with one identified representative set, then ROO-012 records sampling/retest scope. A single set is not a population reliability study.

## Equipment and prepared state

Minimum first-power/recovery setup: adjustable current-limited DC supply, DMM with known burden/fused current ranges, magnification, verified mating cables, USB data cable and host. Native USB requires the target's battery/input supply and accessible RESET/BOOT. J7 additionally requires a 3.3-V UART adapter with safe contact/registration; its pin 2 senses target voltage only.

Waveform checks require a scope with known bandwidth/sample rate, short ground connections and suitably rated probes; use a differential probe or two matched channels referenced to **board GND** and subtraction for the BTL speaker. Never ground OUTP/OUTN through a scope clip. Converter switching edges need a suitable high-bandwidth probing setup; record results as BLOCKED if it cannot resolve the pulse widths being assessed. A logic analyzer cannot establish analog overshoot limits. Load/thermal tests need a rated electronic load or measured power resistors, shunt/current capture and thermocouples; sound characterization needs a calibrated SPL instrument for absolute levels.

First apply power with speaker/display disconnected, verified controls cable OFF (or unplugged), no USB/UART back-power path, and a 4.5-V regulated input at J1 with a **50-mA initial current limit**. This is an engineering short-screen condition, not a normal running-current allowance. Stop and inspect if it remains current-limited. Hold RESET low for the initial 3.3-V check. After that passes, use a **0.75-A input limit at 4.5 V** for the no-audio boot trial, only with leads/connectors rated for it. A trip is inconclusive until startup demand/supply droop is measured; do not increase current blindly. The full-load input ceiling is selected from the exact connector/holder ratings and measured demand in O02/B05; no universal full-load current limit is approved here.

Before adding peripherals, use PCB-specific diagnostic firmware: amp enable18 LOW, SD command21 LOW, I2S47/48/14 LOW while 5 V is off, OLED power41/bus11 LOW, battery-measure12 LOW; RGB HIGH/off after initialization; GPIO7/13/15 inputs. Record reset/boot transients separately because firmware cannot control the interval before execution. Do not flash the old breadboard pin map unchanged.

No whole-board standby µA, ripple mV, sound dB, response milliseconds, battery days or closed-enclosure temperature target is silently selected by this draft.

## B01 — Incoming assembly, isolated cables and unpowered continuity

Equipment: magnification, DMM, selected-part drawings, cable pin map. All power and adapters disconnected; discharge capacitors through an appropriate resistor and confirm zero voltage. Identify parts/DNPs, shorts, solder orientation and board labels. R11 is DNP; J7/TP pads unpopulated; check daughterboard connector back-side orientation. Test every isolated GH/PH cable conductor against its intended mate, and every unintended pair for shorts. Proposed cable screen: corrected end-to-end resistance <1 Ω and isolation >1 MΩ at DMM test voltage; these are wiring screens, not contact-resistance qualification. Check controls ON/common/OFF truth table and each switch return. Check native J1.1→Q1.3 continuity, not a historical pin number; the MOSFET through-path is assessed powered in B02. Record rail-to-ground resistance in both meter polarities after settling; no universal resistance threshold is valid across semiconductors. **PASS:** exact pin mapping/parts/orientation agree, no unintended conductor short or open, and suspicious rail readings are investigated before B02. Supplier drawing gaps are BLOCKED O01/O03.

## B02 — First power, protection path and VBAT regression

Use the prepared 4.5-V/50-mA OFF setup. Record supply output, J1.1, Q1.3, TP2 and input current; then hold RESET low, select ON and measure TP7/TP3. Once stable, release reset using the boot current limit above. Compare J1 lead/contact drop and Q1 drop at idle and a characterized small load. Repeat **10 cold starts** at 4.5 V with rail discharge confirmed between trials. **PASS:** no persistent current limit in the validated setup, correct TP7 behavior, L02 while operating, successful boot 10/10 and no downstream-power workaround required. Drop magnitude/startup time are CHARACTERIZED against actual current until O02 supplies allowances. Do not infer reverse-insertion survival from the Q1 schematic; intentional reverse-battery testing needs a separately bounded procedure after ratings review. ROO-002 supplies the old order/revision if recoverable; this tests today's J1→Q1→VIN path regardless of historical identification.

## B03 — Rail enable, PG and switch shutdown

Equipment: supply, DMM, scope/logic logging; 4.5-V input, initially no audio/display. Capture TP2/3/4/5/6/7/8 at power-up and at five repetitions each of ON, OFF and controls-unplugged states (power down before remating). Command GPIO18 LOW/HIGH/LOW with SD LOW. **PASS:** L06 enable truth table; 3.3 V starts with controls ON; 5 V appears on command, L02/L03 hold when operating; neither PG pulls an MCU input to 5 V. Capture U2 enable as 3.3 V decays and verify it is not held on through external cables. TPS63070 has no active output-discharge option, so instantaneous zero volts after OFF is not required. Record rail decay/PG timing/residual voltage/current after 1, 10 and 60 seconds as CHARACTERIZED. Final nominal rail accuracy follows O01/L04; an unfinalized band cannot PASS.

## B04 — USB and UART programming/recovery

Equipment: verified data cable/host, target supply, DMM; 3.3-V UART adapter and stable J7 contact if testing UART. First check J7.2 target voltage, common ground and adapter output voltage; adapter TX→J7.4, RX←J7.3; never feed target power through J7.2. Record adapter/driver/tool versions and board/FQBN/flash settings. With powered target, perform **three flash/verify/reset/boot cycles** through the selected service route, including one manual BOOT-low/reset/release recovery. Confirm GPIO7 LOW without USB and HIGH with VBUS while the target is powered. Test USB attached with J1 supply removed and controls OFF: characterize TP3/TP4/TP2 residuals and verify no normal boot/charging operation. **PASS:** reproducible selected-route flash verification and recovery, correct powered USB detection, no unsupported external voltage on target pins. UART access can remain BLOCKED until fixture exists if bare-board USB recovery works; assembled J7 access remains B15/ROO-016. Explicitly record which route was actually tested.

## B05 — Load, battery sag and startup margin

Equipment: rated supply/leads, DMM, scope, electronic load or measured resistors, current capture. Start at 4.5 V; test 3.3-V total loads near **0.1/0.25/0.5 A**, and commanded 5-V total loads near **0.1/0.3/0.6 A**, within actual sourcing limits. These are characterization points from the engineering envelope, not guaranteed output ratings. Account for native load; do not blindly add 0.5 A on top of MCU current. Measure each rail separately, then combined at permitted load, initially 10 seconds per point, extending to 60 seconds only after stable operation. Record actual voltage/current/power, ripple, PG, reset logs and thermal trend. At representative load, examine PFET at approximately **5.4/4.5/3.3/3.0 V**, only within the frozen cell/component envelope; separate already-running ramps from discharged cold starts, 10 starts per chosen guaranteed-start point. Use an emulator for low-voltage exploration; do not force real cells below their allowed discharge condition. **PASS electrical subchecks:** L02/L03 during operating trials and no unexplained reset at a frozen required load. Startup below L01, maximum useful load, transient response, efficiency and cutoff are CHARACTERIZED until D06/O02 limits are chosen. A test point beyond a verified current/thermal rating is BLOCKED, not attempted.

At safe low/mid/high measured loads, capture each converter's L1/L2 switching-node maximum, minimum and excursion duration using the qualified probing setup, first checking for probe-induced ringing. Compare against the exact S01 voltage/time-dependent stress table with instrument uncertainty; stop on a credible violation. Absolute maxima are stress boundaries, not design-margin targets. Record switching stress as CHARACTERIZED pending an engineering margin decision; insufficient bandwidth makes this subcheck BLOCKED. Use a separate bandwidth-limited rail-ripple capture and state the bandwidth for both measurements.

## B06 — Audio rail, BTL output and mute sequencing

Equipment: target supply, scope with valid differential measurement, 8-Ω rated dummy load then exact speaker, audio diagnostic. At 4.5-V input, start with low digital amplitude and left-channel tone; GPIO21 LOW, enable18 HIGH, observe PG/rail, start valid I2S47/48/14 then assert21. Confirm right-only data is not selected by the configured left-channel mode. Shutdown by lowering21, stopping/holding I2S LOW, then disabling18. Repeat **20 cycles**; repeat useful levels after B05 establishes supply margin. **PASS:** correct channel/output pair, commanded audible tone and mute without reset or operating rail-limit excursion, no high I2S drive into the unpowered amp. Capture differential audio, DC behavior, clipping, startup/shutdown transient, amp-off rail leakage and current. Peak SPL, distortion, pop audibility, mute latency and maximum sustainable amplitude are CHARACTERIZED; do not declare them acceptable without D06. Never use ground-referenced speaker wiring.

## B07 — OLED cycling and bus isolation

Equipment: exact four-pin SH1106 module/socket, supply/DMM, scope or logic analyzer, diagnostic logging RTC and display. At 4.5-V input, isolate GPIO11 LOW; power41 HIGH; measure module startup/rail and wait the manufacturer's required power-up interval. With the shared bus idle, connect11 HIGH, then send the SH1106 initialization and display commands. Record and freeze the exact delays in ROO-011 rather than guess them. Before power-off, finish transactions, isolate11 LOW, then power41 LOW. Run **100 cycles**, with at least 1 second showing border/checkerboard/full-white patterns and at least 1 second off; lengthen for required module timing. Read RTC every cycle with display off. **PASS:** 100/100 successful starts/patterns and RTC reads, correct pin order, no stuck shared I2C bus or reset. Measure supply and pullups on both sides plus current in off/full-white states; characterize off-rail decay/leakage and delay margins. If required module timing/voltage bounds are unavailable, final display acceptance is BLOCKED O03, even if a demonstration works.

## B08 — RTC timekeeping, interrupt and sleep wake

Equipment: target supply, reference clock with known resolution, diagnostic log, scope/logic analyzer on GPIO1. Display isolated/off, audio off, 3.3 V continuously supplied. Read/write time and round-trip all fields; log RTC identity/configuration. Configure ten one-minute timer/alarm wake trials, clearing flags between trials, and sleep the MCU between events. Proposed test abort is 90 seconds per trial; this is an observation window, not a product alarm latency target. **PASS:** 10/10 interrupts/wakes, flags clear and interrupt releases, monotonic RTC time across MCU reset/sleep with power maintained. Characterize time from interrupt to firmware event separately. Then compare elapsed RTC time over **24 hours at recorded room temperature**, including reference/start-stop uncertainty; drift is CHARACTERIZED until a defensible selected-part/temperature acceptance interval is set. No ±1 ppm claim across all temperatures is made.

## B09 — Sleep, standby and loss of time

Equipment: low-burden current measurement or shunt/current analyzer, scope for bursts, supply/DMM. Use 4.5 V and repeat at O02-selected input endpoints. Measure: running idle; display off/audio off/radio configured awake; MCU deep sleep with RTC wake armed; controls OFF; controls cable absent. For each state settle 60 seconds and record **five minutes** of input current/energy plus TP2/3/4; remove USB/UART and avoid autorange transients. Verify sleep GPIO defaults and wake sources; distinguish MCU deep sleep (RTC rail alive) from hardware standby (3.3 V decays). Power cycle sufficiently to lose RTC supply and inspect the RTC validity/status before setting time again. **PASS functional:** expected wake under maintained power and diagnostic reports power-loss/time-invalid evidence rather than asserting preserved wall-clock time. Final app response is D05, not invented here. All current/battery-life figures are CHARACTERIZED until D06's operating duty cycle and endurance target exist. Typical individual-IC µA numbers do not set a whole-board limit.

## B10 — Buttons, maximum harness and bounce

Equipment: both final-length cables plus a verified 200-mm cable sample within contract, DMM, scope/logic/event log. At 4.5 V, press/release each of VOL−, MODE, VOL+, BAT **20 times**; include brief, ordinary and held presses. Check exact physical labels/functions and LOW/HIGH against L05 at R50–R53 MCU-side pads. Capture contact bounce/undershoot/overshoot with at least 10-ms software debounce configured and recorded. **PASS:** one press/release event per trial, no cross-trigger/stuck input, correct held logic and no input-rating excursion at the validated probe bandwidth. A waveform the scope cannot resolve is INCONCLUSIVE. Debounce length may need adjustment from the initial contract; application repeat behavior/eligibility-crossing override semantics belong to ROO-001/006/015. Test bare switches first; cap tolerance and actuator overtravel belong to B15.

## B11 — RGB mapping/current

Equipment: DMM, target supply, diagnostic, selected LED data. Verify common anode on J6.2, red/green/blue on J6.4/.5/.6. Command each channel alone at 0/25/50/100% duty then all off, three repetitions. Use resistor drop across R28/29/30 to derive channel current with measured resistance, accounting for PWM method. **PASS:** correct color responds active LOW, no omitted series resistor, current stays within the exact LED/channel and MCU sink ratings from O01/O03, all-off command works. Perceived brightness, diffusion and nighttime suitability are CHARACTERIZED for D06/ROO-016; a nominal resistor value alone cannot establish actual current or usability.

## B12 — Switched battery measurement

Equipment: calibrated DMM at TP2, ADC diagnostic, high-impedance scope at C34. At approximately 3.0/4.5/5.4 V on PFET (within frozen envelope and after stable startup), enable12; capture ADC-node settling; sample at 1/5/10/20 ms; record at least 20 averaged calibrated readings per point and the selected ADC attenuation. Compare `Vadc ×4.3` with simultaneous TP2 voltage at idle and a known load. Disable12 and record residual at 10/100/1000 ms plus input-current change. **PASS functional:** divider enables/disables, approaches measured divider ratio, input remains in ADC ratings and no input overstress/reset. ADC accuracy, usable settling delay and leakage are CHARACTERIZED until uncertainty/calibration allowances are frozen. Compare TP2 with J1 to explain protection/wire loss; this is not an automatic battery-percentage calibration.

## B13 — Sustained alarm power, temperature and battery interval

Equipment: exact batteries/holder/speaker/harness, current/voltage logging and temperature probes; then actual closed enclosure after B15. Begin with 10-minute open-bench playback using frozen waveform/gain; extend to 30 minutes only within previously established component/lead limits. Log input/rail minima, current/energy, case/PCB/cell temperature, ambient and resets at least once per second where feasible, with scope capture for faster rail dips. Repeat closed enclosure only after open-bench behavior and physical assembly are sound. Package surface temperature is not junction temperature. Establish test-stop limits from exact parts/thermal assessment before running. **Electrical pass** remains operating rails/no reset at the selected required condition; thermal trend and achievable playback/endurance are CHARACTERIZED. The final multi-day experiment needs D06's duty cycle, batteries, environment, minimum remaining alarm capability and acceptable interval. No continuous 0.6-A audio-load or days-of-life guarantee follows from the saved simulation.

## B14 — Sound, latency and radio characterization

Equipment: calibrated SPL meter for absolute sound, sound/radio diagnostic with monotonic event timestamps, paired known sensor or radio test peer. At the actual bedside location record microphone distance/height/orientation, room/noise, gain/waveform and battery/load state. Take three 30-second samples of normal and fallback patterns; report weighting/time response and peak/equivalent level. Measure silence/restart command-to-audio latency with synchronized event/audio capture, 20 repetitions. At intended bedside/sensor positions and representative door states, send 100 numbered test packets each direction and report receipt, loss and latency distribution. **CHARACTERIZED only:** D06 supplies sound/latency/range targets and ROO-006 supplies application semantics. Sensor absence/failure/grace cases are not passed by a radio ping. A phone recording may document sound but cannot replace calibrated SPL evidence.

## B15 — Fit, actuation and service access

Equipment: actual enclosure/fasteners/cables/parts, calipers/feeler gauges and suitable actuator travel/force measurement, service fixture if available. Compare actual part drawing envelopes to board positions before forcing an assembly. Assemble with intended cable bends and mounting hardware; test each cap and slide switch 20 times; measure free play, travel to actuation/release, overtravel stop and return. **PASS functional sample:** no preload/stuck button, reliable actuation/release, no connector/pin/glass strain, accessible assembly/recovery route. The nominal **0.055-mm** front stem gap supplies no guaranteed tolerance margin. Final force/travel/durability criteria and process tolerances are ROO-016/D09; record them before dimensional qualification. For J7, verify pin registration and retention through the allotted corridor while powered only by the target source, then repeat B04 via the fixture. Bare-board success does not pass enclosed service access.
